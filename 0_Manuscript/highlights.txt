Highlights

- PRISMA systematic review of 263 deep learning brain MRI reconstruction studies
- Only 18 of 263 studies pair a fidelity metric with a reader on the same data
- Erasing a 5 mm3 lesion moves global PSNR by roughly a thousandth of a decibel
- Reader evaluation dropped from 32.4% to 18.2%, recovering to 20.7% in recent era
- No public dataset in the corpus is curated for acute stroke or haemorrhage
